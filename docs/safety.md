# Safety

**This system is an assistive tool. It must not replace the user's judgment, a companion, or on-site traffic safety checks.**

Computer vision can misclassify or miss signals and crossings. Results may change with lighting, weather, occlusion, camera angle, camera movement, image quality, model training data, local signal design, and traffic conditions. No accuracy or safe-crossing guarantee is established in this repository.

Do not use an unverified prototype as the sole basis for entering a road. Confirm the physical signal and surrounding traffic using appropriate accessible methods and support. Stop use if cues are delayed, contradictory, unavailable, or inconsistent with the environment. Hardware power, battery handling, and motor-driver safety also require build-specific instructions before use.
