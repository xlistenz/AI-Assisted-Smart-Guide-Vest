## What the awards demonstrate

The awards recognize the project's innovation and presentation in the 2026 competitions. Alongside the event photos, they support the project's promise and early-stage feasibility as a student-built assistive-technology prototype. They are not clinical or field-safety evaluations, product certification, or proof that the system can independently guide a person across a road.
